## 1. Overview & Purpose

Depoii is a data engine purpose-built for commercial roofing estimators. It replaces manual administrative record-pulling by cross-referencing county appraisal rolls (DCAD/TAD structural footprints and deed owners), municipal building inspection portals (permitted reroof history), and NOAA SPC severe storm hail swaths onto a single, audit-verified property dossier.

The **Design-Partner Pilot** is an invitation-only program for established North Texas commercial roofing contractors. It provides access to Depoii's property intelligence and custom dossiers at zero cost in exchange for weekly operational feedback.

## 2. What Depoii Delivers (Our Commitment)

1. **Immediate First Dossier (Within 24 Hours):**
   - A fully field-verified commercial property dossier in your primary operating territory.
   - Includes structural improvement footprint, municipal permit history, NOAA hail date/distance, and two-lane contact mapping (Deed Owner Principal vs. On-Site Operating Facility Tenant).
2. **Two Additional Territory Dossiers Across the 30 Days:**
   - 2 additional verified commercial properties tailored to your exact target parameters (e.g. industrial warehouses $>50,000$ sq ft, aged low-slope roofs, specific DFW sub-markets).
3. **Dedicated Estimator Workspace Access:**
   - Single-user access for your lead estimator or commercial division manager.
   - Access to verified property dossiers, structural TAD/DCAD footprints, municipal permit history, NOAA SPC hail distance, and two-lane contact maps.
4. **Protected Exclusivity Policy:**
   - Every property dossier delivered to you carries **100% exclusive protection for 7 days**. We never double-sell or cross-pitch active properties to competing roofers.

## 3. What We Ask from You (Your Commitment)

1. **Active Estimator Evaluation:**

2. **One 20-Minute Feedback Call Per Week (or Email Audit):**
   - A weekly 20-minute check-in with the founder to critique the data:
     - *Did the structural footprint match the real roof deck?*

   - Tell us what breaks, what is missing, and what data fields your estimators actually need to bid a reroof.
